USD to Robux Calculator

Convert an amount in the currency you pick below into Robux. The Robux rate is editable, so you can use the DevEx cash-out rate, an approximate buying price, or your own rate.

Rate basis: the default $3.80 per 1,000 R$ is the DevEx cash-out rate (US$0.0038 per R$). The buying price preset is approximate: the price you pay for Robux varies by package and platform, so treat $12.50 per 1,000 R$ as a rough guide, not an exact quote.

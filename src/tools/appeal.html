Appeal Generator

Staring at a moderation message and not sure what to write? Pick what happened, add your username, and get a calm, polite appeal draft you can copy and send to Roblox Support.

Your appeal draft

This is only a draft for you to copy. Each draft is written fresh by a writing service when you press the button, and nothing you type here is stored. If the writing service is busy, no draft appears and you can simply try again in a moment. Appeals themselves go through the official Roblox support page at roblox.com/support.

How much is that outfit actually worth?

Know-how · 5 November 2026 · 3 min read

Outfits are more than a look

A saved outfit can quietly hold hundreds or thousands of Robux worth of items. A hat here, a limited there, and suddenly a casual avatar is wearing serious money. Most people never add it up.

How the pricing works

Every item gets priced on its own. Stuff that is on sale uses its current catalog price. Free items count as zero, because they are free. Off-sale items use their last known price. Limiteds with resellers use the lowest resale price, which is the number that actually moves with the market.

The honest gaps

Some items have no price data at all: old bundle parts, items Roblox never indexed, things that were never sold separately. Those show up as No price data instead of a made-up number. A total with a couple of unpriced items is a floor, not the full story.

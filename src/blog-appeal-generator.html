Appeal Generator: the full guide

Tool guide · Player · 6 November 2026 · 4 min read

What it does

Getting moderated is stressful enough without having to write the appeal from a blank page. This generator turns your situation into a calm, polite draft you can copy and send to Roblox Support. It covers the four cases people ask about most: a terminated account, a warning that does not make sense, a deleted item, and being locked out of an account you own.

How to use it

Type your Roblox username. The draft is addressed from that account, so spelling matters here.

Pick the appeal type that matches your situation, then add a short note about what happened if you want to. That note becomes the facts of your case in the letter.

Press Generate Draft, read it through, and use the Copy draft button. Then paste it into the official Roblox support form and send it from there.

Reading the result

Each request gets its own wording, written fresh for your situation. A termination appeal asks for a manual review and a second chance. A warning appeal asks what the warning was actually about. An item appeal asks which rule the item broke and whether it can be restored. A login appeal asks for help recovering access through the official verification process, and it never asks anyone for a password.

Good to know

The generator only writes the text. It cannot send an appeal and it cannot promise an outcome. What you type is sent only to a writing service that drafts the letter for you, and it is not stored. Every draft is written fresh for your request. If that service is busy, no draft is generated and you can simply try again a moment later. Roblox decides every appeal, and honest, specific appeals written in your own situation stand a better chance than angry ones. Never share your password or cookie with anyone who offers to appeal for you.

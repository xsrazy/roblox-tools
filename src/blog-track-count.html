User Tracker: Count: the full guide

Tool guide · Tracking · 5 November 2026 · 4 min read

What it does

A small stat with a satisfying number: how many users you track, and their combined follower total. Your watch list's combined reach, in one figure.

How to use it

Open the User Tracker; the count sits with your list.

It updates itself as you add and remove people.

Reading the result

Combined followers double-counts people who follow several of your tracked accounts, so read it as a fun scale indicator, not a unique-audience report. Still, watching the combined total climb over months is its own little reward.

Good to know

No separate setup: the count is computed from the list you already keep. Empty list, zero count, honest as always.

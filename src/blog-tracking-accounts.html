Watching an account change, without living on the refresh button

Tips · 5 November 2026 · 3 min read

Numbers move quietly

An account you checked last month is not the same account today. Followers went up (or down), the avatar changed, a new limited showed up in the RAP. Checking by hand means remembering the old numbers yourself, and nobody does that for long.

A watchlist that stays yours

The User Tracker keeps the accounts you care about in a list stored in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account to create. Open it whenever, run a check, and it shows how each account's numbers compare with your last look.

Good uses for it

Traders watch their own RAP drift. Group owners keep an eye on admins. Friends just like seeing who finally hit a follower milestone. It is a small tool, but it beats a spreadsheet full of screenshots.

The honest limit

The tracker checks when you ask it to. It is not a background alarm and it will not ping you at 3 AM because someone gained ten followers. Open the page, press check, see what changed. Simple as that.

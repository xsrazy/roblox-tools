Privacy Policy

What this site does and does not do with your data.

On this page

Last updated 5 November 2026.

No accounts, no passwords

There are no accounts and no sign-in on this site. We never ask for your Roblox password or cookies, and you should never type them into a site that does.

What gets recorded

Public usernamesLatest 12 shown

When you check a username, that username and the tool you used appear on the Recently looked up page. It's public profile data, the same anyone can see on roblox.com. The public page only ever shows the latest 12 lookups; older ones roll off it.

Stays in your browser

The User Tracker stores its list in your own browser (localStorage). It never leaves your device, there is no copy on our side, and clearing your browser data clears it for good.

Server logs

Like most sites, the hosting keeps basic server logs, such as which pages were visited and when, to keep things running and spot abuse.

We don't sell data

We don't sell data, and there is no advertising tracker business here. If that ever changes, this page changes first.

Questions

Anything unclear? Ask through the Contact page and we will sort it out.

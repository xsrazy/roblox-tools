User Tracker: Remove All: the full guide

Tool guide · Tracking · 5 November 2026 · 4 min read

What it does

The full reset: clear your entire track list at once, every user and every snapshot, when you want to start the watch list from a clean slate.

How to use it

Open the User Tracker.

Press Remove All; the button asks for one more press to confirm, right there on the button.

Press again within a few seconds and the list is empty.

Reading the result

The two-step press exists because this deletes everything at once, and the old popup-confirm approach silently failed in some browsers. This way the confirmation always works, and an accidental single tap can never wipe your tracking history.

Good to know

Only your local list is cleared: nothing on Roblox changes, nobody is unfollowed, no accounts are touched. It is your browser storage being emptied, that is all.

Guides

Step-by-step tutorials for the jobs people actually come here to do. Each one pairs the tool with the guide that explains its results.

Inventory

Check someone’s RAP

RAP is the total recent average price of a user’s limiteds. The checker scans their collectibles and adds it up live. A dash means the inventory is private, not that the tool broke.

Open the RAP Checker · Read the RAP guide
Player

Price a saved outfit, item by item

Pick a user, pick one of their saved outfits, and every item gets priced from the live catalog: on-sale price, last price for off-sale items, lowest resale for limiteds. The total can move as resale prices move.

Open the Outfit Value Estimator · Read its full guide
Player

Save a profile card as an image

Run a lookup on the homepage and press Download card inside the result. You get a framed PNG with the avatar, stats and badges, ready to post anywhere. The web address only appears on the downloaded copy, not on screen.

Open the Account Checker · Read the profile card post
Tracking

Track an account over time

Add the accounts you care about once, then check them whenever you like. The tracker shows how friends, followers and RAP moved since your last check, and the whole list stays in your browser.

Open the User Tracker · Read the tracking post
Player

See if a username is taken

Thinking of a new name? The checker tells you right away whether a username is available, already taken, or not allowed, before you waste time in the signup or rename flow.

Open the Username Checker · Read its full guide
Renders

Download a clean avatar picture

Sometimes you only need the face. Ava Downloader gives you the headshot on its own, while Avatar Viewer shows full body, bust and headshot together with body colors and scales.

Open the Ava Downloader · Read its full guide
Games

Check a game before you join

Game Info shows players right now, visits, votes and favorites. Game Servers goes one step further and lists the live public servers with player counts, ping and FPS, up to a couple of hundred servers.

Open the Game Info · Read its full guide
Games & Groups

Look into a group properly

Group Check pulls the member count, owner and description from a group link or ID. Group Item Scan then checks whether a user owns anything that group made, merch and uniforms included.

Open the Group Check · Read its full guide
Calculators

Convert Robux, and dodge the tax surprise

The calculators convert Robux to USD and 15 other currencies at the DevEx rate or your own rate. Selling something? The Tax Calculator works out the 30 percent marketplace fee both ways, so the number you receive is not a surprise.

Open the Robux Calculator · Read the tax guide
Compare

Compare two accounts side by side

Two accounts, one table: friends, followers, RAP and join dates in matching columns so the differences jump out. The Stat Graph turns the same numbers into bars when you want the quick visual version.

Open the Compare Users · Read its full guide
Inventory

Find one specific item in an inventory

One question, one answer: does this user own this item? Item Checker looks directly, and UAID Lookup goes deeper, down to the individual copy of a limited and its serial where there is one.

Open the Item Checker · Read its full guide
Renders

Render an outfit as one picture

Outfit Render presents a single saved outfit properly: the full render plus the item sheet under it, downloadable as one PNG. The Outfits Gallery does the same for the whole wardrobe at once.

Open the Outfit Render · Read its full guide

49 tools, one toolbox: Roblox Tools is live

News · 5 November 2026 · 2 min read

Why build another toolbox?

Roblox data is scattered. One site checks RAP, another renders avatars, a third does Robux math, and half of them want you to sign in first. This site puts the lot in one place, in your browser, for free.

What is in the box

Profile lookups with a downloadable card. RAP and inventory checks, down to individual items and UAIDs. Outfit tools, including a value estimator that prices every item from the live catalog. Avatar renders in every flavor, Robux calculators with real exchange rates, a user tracker, and a public page of what people are looking up right now.

Built in the open

The Changelog tracks what changes and when, the System Status page runs a live check in front of you, and the FAQ answers the awkward questions too, like why a private inventory shows a dash.

What is next

More guides like this one, and fixes wherever people trip up. If a tool gives you a weird result, the fastest way to get it sorted is the Contact page. Screenshots help.

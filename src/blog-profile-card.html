Turn any profile into a card you can share

Tips · 5 November 2026 · 2 min read

Links are easy to ignore

You can paste a roblox.com profile link anywhere, but nobody sees anything until they click it. A card shows the good stuff up front: avatar, username, friends, followers, join date and whether the inventory is public.

Make one in under a minute

Go to the Account Checker on the homepage, type the username and hit Check. The card builds itself from live data. Then press Download card and you get a tidy PNG, ready for Discord, WhatsApp or wherever your group hangs out.

One small detail

The web address only appears on the downloaded image, not on the screen version. That way screenshots of the page stay clean, and the shared PNG still says where it came from.

Works for any public account

Yours, a friend's, a famous trader's. If the profile is public on Roblox, the card can be built. Private inventory sections simply show as private, same as they do for everyone.

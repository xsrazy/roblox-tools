Bundle Render: the full guide

Tool guide · Renders & Templates · 5 November 2026 · 4 min read

What it does

Bundles hide their contents behind one picture. This tool renders the bundle and lists what is actually inside it: every body part and item the package includes.

How to use it

Paste the bundle link or type the bundle ID.

Press Render and read the contents list under the picture.

Reading the result

Knowing the contents matters because bundles are bought whole: the render shows the combined character, the list shows the pieces, including the head, torso, arms, legs and any extras, so you know exactly what your Robux buys.

Good to know

Some bundles include items that are also sold separately and some that are bundle-only; the contents list is the ground truth either way.
